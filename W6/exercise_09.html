The Quokka

The quokka is a small marsupial native
to Australia. It is well known for its
friendly appearance and often looks
like it is smiling. Quokkas usually live
on islands and coastal areas, and they
are most active at night.

Where They Live

Quokkas are mainly found on
Rottnest Island and some other
nearby islands in Western
Australia. They live in bushland,
grasslands, and local coastal areas
where there is plenty of food
and shelter.
